Metro-shuttle Flutter Application
Overview
Metro-shuttle is a children's transportation application designed to make commutes safer and more reliable. The app offers real-time tracking, instant notifications, and secure check-in/check-out processes, ensuring parents are well-informed about their child's transportation status. Metro-shuttle addresses the concerns of parents by providing a comprehensive solution for monitoring and managing their children's transportation, ultimately reducing anxiety and enhancing safety.

Project Goals
The primary goals of Metro-shuttle include:

Enhance Safety: Implement secure check-in and check-out methods to ensure children are only picked up and dropped off by authorized personnel.
Increase Transparency: Provide real-time tracking and notifications so parents can monitor their child's location and trip progress.
Simplify Monitoring: Develop an easy-to-use interface that allows parents to quickly check and manage their child's transportation schedule.
Improve Communication: Facilitate reliable communication between parents and transport providers.
Reduce Parental Anxiety: Offer peace of mind to parents with detailed trip histories and real-time updates.
Ensure Reliability: Ensure consistent and dependable transportation services, helping children arrive on time.
Foster a Safe Environment: Continuously improve security measures to maintain high safety standards.
Expand Reach: Grow the app's user base to provide safe and reliable transportation to more families.
Features
1. User Management
Account Creation and Management: Users can create accounts using their phone numbers, update personal information, and manage profiles. Roles are assigned to differentiate between parents, drivers, and coordinators with specific permissions.
Authentication: Secure login/logout functionality and password recovery via SMS verification are provided.
2. Child and Trip Management
Child Profiles: Parents can create and edit profiles for their children, including contact information and emergency details.
Manage Pick-Up/Drop-Off Locations: Designated pick-up and drop-off locations can be set and modified by parents.
Trip Scheduling: Parents can schedule regular or one-time trips for their children, view trip details, and update or cancel trips as needed.
3. Real-Time Tracking and Notifications
Live Map View: A live map displays the current location of the shuttle and the child.
Trip Notifications: Alerts are sent to parents when their child is picked up, dropped off, or if there are any delays.
4. Security and Access Control
Secure Check-In/Check-Out: Only authorized personnel can pick up or drop off children using secure check-in/check-out procedures.
Verification: Verification methods such as PINs confirm the identity of the person picking up or dropping off the child.
Data Security: Encryption is used to protect sensitive user data, and access controls ensure privacy and security.
5. Support and Communication
Customer Support: Users can contact customer support via email, phone, or in-app messaging.
FAQ and Help Resources: A comprehensive FAQ section and help resources assist users with common issues.
